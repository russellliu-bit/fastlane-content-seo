## 1. Purpose
This document defines the current content and newsroom architecture of Heyup.

## 2. Role of Content
Content is one of the central explanation and discovery layers of the platform.
It supports:
- product understanding
- trend storytelling
- campaign amplification
- review credibility
- buying guidance
- brand visibility

## 3. Active Blog / Content Channels
Observed channels include:
- Tech News
- Hunts & News Hunt
- Product Reviews
- Heyup Programs & Buyer's Guide
- Brand Buzz
- Heyup Trend & Insight Lab
- Feature Stories
- Heyup Content Creator Program

## 4. Content Functions by Channel
Tech News:
- tryout updates
- tech trends
- product-related news

Hunts & News Hunt:
- curated news and editorial selections

Product Reviews:
- in-depth reviews and hands-on style content

Heyup Programs & Buyer's Guide:
- program explainers
- shopping guides
- recommendation content

Brand Buzz:
- brand-specific updates and announcements

Trend & Insight Lab:
- market/trend analysis
- higher-level insight pieces

Feature Stories:
- event coverage
- broader editorial storytelling

Content Creator Program:
- creator-facing updates and program content

## 6. Strategic Interpretation
The content architecture is already broader than a standard blog.
It supports editorial, commercial, program, brand, and community functions simultaneously.

## 7. Implications
- Future content strategy should map content types explicitly to business goals
- News, reviews, guides, campaigns, and brand content should be modeled as distinct formats
- Affiliate-content initiatives should likely sit inside or adjacent to the buyer’s guide / review structure, not overwrite all existing content logic
