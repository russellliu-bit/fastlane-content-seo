## 1. Public Business Role
Heyup presents itself as a community-driven content and commerce platform that helps users discover and evaluate products while helping innovative brands reach interested audiences.

## 2. Publicly Visible Value to Users
- Discover new and innovative tech
- Join a community of like-minded enthusiasts
- Access tryouts and early product experiences
- Read or create authentic product-related content
- Shop selected products

## 3. Publicly Visible Value to Brands
- Reach an audience already interested in cool tech
- Build awareness and buzz
- Collect reviews and feedback from real users
- Use Heyup as a launch/discovery channel
- Collaborate with creators and ambassadors

## 4. Publicly Visible Value to Creators and Partners
- Publish content to a global audience
- Collaborate with brands
- Access products early
- Join ambassador programs
- Join affiliate promotion programs

## 5. Inferred Business Model Components
Based on public pages, Heyup appears to combine:
- community growth
- content and review participation
- product discovery
- commerce/store activity
- brand partnership programs
- affiliate/creator partnership programs
