## 1. One-line Description
Heyup is a community-driven content and commerce platform for cool tech discovery, authentic reviews, and product participation.

## 2. Expanded Description
Heyup positions itself publicly as a community-led platform where users can discover innovative tech products, share authentic reviews, exchange ideas with other users, and in some cases test products or shop selected items. It also presents itself as a market entry point for innovative brands that want to reach interested audiences.

## 3. Core Public-Facing Modules
- Community
- Tryouts
- Newsroom
- Store
- Ambassador Program
- Content Creator Program
- Launchpad
- Affiliate Program

## 4. What Heyup Appears to Do
Heyup connects tech enthusiasts, creators, and brands through content, product discovery, product testing, feedback, and commerce-oriented experiences.

## 5. Public Mission Signals
- Discover innovative tech products

- Encourage user exchange and participation
- Build an extended playground for empowered consumers
- Help innovative brands reach interested audiences
