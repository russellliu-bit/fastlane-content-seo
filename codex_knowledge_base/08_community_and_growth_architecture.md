# Community and Growth Architecture

Status: draft
Owner: Russell
Last Updated: 2026-03-07
Scope: current state
Source of Truth: internal Shopify scan

## 1. Purpose
This document defines the current community and growth architecture of Heyup.

## 2. Role of Community
Community is a structural layer of the platform, not just a marketing add-on.
It supports:
- user participation
- product demand signaling
- tryout eligibility
- tester announcements
- review generation
- brand-specific conversations

## 3. Community Surfaces
Observed community-related surfaces include:
- Heyup Community
- Discord
- brand-specific community channels

## 4. Community Features
Observed features include:
- membership requirement for tryout applications
- like/vote system
- Community Favorites collection
- All-Time Top Products by Likes collection
- tester selection announcements
- review submission system

## 5. Community Programs
Observed programs include:
- Heyup Ambassador Program
- Content Creator Program
- Tryout tester program

## 6. Customer / Audience Segments
Observed segmentation examples include:
- email subscribers
- abandoned checkouts
- purchase history tiers
- B2B customers
- tryout winners
- subscribed customers

## 7. Internal Interpretation
Community is one of the main trust and retention engines in the platform.
It influences which products get attention, who gets access, and what user-generated content is produced.

## 8. Implications
- Future projects should specify whether they consume or generate community signals
- Community-derived metrics should be reusable in product ranking, content personalization, and lifecycle prioritization
- Membership state and participation state may need to become reusable entities in future data models
