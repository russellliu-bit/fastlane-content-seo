# Heyup Brand Voice and Positioning

Status: draft
Owner: Russell
Last Updated: 2026-03-07
Scope: current state + recommended interpretation
Source of Truth: public-facing official website, with internal interpretation noted

## 1. Brand Positioning
Heyup is positioned as a community-led playground for cool tech, where users discover innovative products, share authentic opinions, and influence what gets attention next.

## 2. Brand Promise
Heyup helps people discover new tech earlier, evaluate it more transparently, and participate more actively than on a traditional review site or store.

## 3. Tone Keywords
- Friendly
- Playful
- Community-first
- Transparent
- Enthusiast-driven
- Forward-looking
- Accessible, not overly corporate

## 4. Voice Characteristics
- Invites participation
- Uses energetic and welcoming language
- Frames users as contributors, not just consumers
- Emphasizes honesty, real feedback, and discovery
- Feels like a playground/community, not a formal lab

## 5. Positioning Differentiators
- Community participation over one-way publishing
- Authentic user reviews over purely editorial claims
- Early product discovery and tryouts
- Bridge between brands and real users
- Combines content, feedback, and commerce

## 6. What the Brand Should Avoid
- Overly polished corporate language
- Hard-sell e-commerce copy as default voice
- Generic gadget-media tone without community angle
- Claims of authority without community proof or user evidence
